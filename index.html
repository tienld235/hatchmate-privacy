Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

HatchMate (by TieChan Software) helps you track eggs in your incubator: day count, turning, candling and hatch-day reminders. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. Google AdMob, which shows the ads, does collect some data — see Advertising below.

Data stored on your device

The batches you create (bird, set date, number of eggs, candling marks, turning log, hatch results), your language, temperature unit and reminder settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive a copy. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your own device backup so it can be restored on a new phone; that backup belongs to your Google account and we cannot access it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders (turning, candling, stop-turning and hatch day) on your phone. They are created and shown by your phone only; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Permissions

Notifications: to remind you when to turn and candle the eggs. Internet: only for ads. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after you save a candling or add a batch, and a short ad you can choose to watch when you add a third batch while two are already running. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and technical information about your device. This is handled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

What Google may collect

An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name); your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location; app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views; and diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate. Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections.

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data security

HatchMate has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your data anywhere. Your batches and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your batches stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app; each of these deletes them immediately. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

Incubation guidance

Temperatures, humidity ranges and day counts in the app are typical values from university extension guides and incubator makers. Always follow your incubator manual.

Children

This app is rated for users 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
